Mongkok Duel · Mong Tong × Gong Gong Gong
Chapter Two · Our closest creative reference

The soundtrack to a lost martial-arts film

Created in 2023 with Beijing psychedelic band Gong Gong Gong, Mongkok Duel was conceived as the soundtrack to a martial-arts film that never existed. Written and recorded live at President Guitar in Mong Kok, Hong Kong, then released on vinyl and toured across Taiwan and Hong Kong.

Howling guitars, rumbling bass, and dramatic keyboard lines move between Motorik rhythms and drone. Chivalry, revenge, and ambushes on every side: with instrumental music alone, we have already told a martial-arts story.
